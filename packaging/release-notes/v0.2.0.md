## What's new in 0.2.0: Frozen Trail

ARC Raiders 2.0 is out, and ARClens caught up.

- **Pendola Pass.** The new map is known everywhere: its name read off the
  in-game map, its place names (markers follow the map), its conditions
  (Redirection, and the ARC Frigate on five maps) and its loot zones. Its
  map image in the Map tab comes once RaidTheory publishes it.
- **New items and quests before the dataset catches up.** Items and
  quests the main dataset doesn't have yet are filled in from MetaForge,
  and items learn where they drop.
- **Item encyclopedia.** The Items tab shows everything about an item:
  where it's found and what drops it, stats, crafting, recycling,
  upgrades, traders, what needs it. Filter by rarity and type; click any
  item it mentions.
- **Where to look.** The game's loot zones and their loot types (Railyard:
  Industrial, Mechanical) tell you where an item turns up; **Show on map**
  lights up its pickups, containers and zones.
- **Condition alerts.** "Alert me" on a condition: a desktop notification
  and a notice in game 5 minutes before it starts.
- **Raid prep on the loading screen.** While the game loads into a raid,
  a card lists what to look for (with icons, have / need and where), and
  the conditions running.
- **Planner.** Pick a station's next level or an item to craft: what it
  takes, what your stash covers, what to craft from parts, what's missing,
  and which spare items to recycle for the missing parts.
- **Stash.** KEEP cards say "Need 12 · 4 in stash · 8 to go". A "Make
  room" list shows what can go to free slots. Items show their value per
  slot.
- **Blueprint hunt list.** Blueprints you haven't learned come first, with
  what each unlocks.
- **Start-up screen**, and the app opens on the saved data at once while
  it refreshes in the background.
- **Fixes.**
  - Hovering the map panel took the keyboard: the game lost focus and
    KDE showed the taskbar, so the map and its markers went away. The
    panel now takes the keyboard only when clicked, and the map stays
    while the pointer is on it.
  - "Refreshing game data…" never cleared; a failed schedule waited 30
    minutes to retry (now 2) and wiped the schedule already shown; Home
    said "Loading" forever when the schedule was offline.

**Upgrading the Flatpak:** quit ARClens first (`flatpak kill
io.github.Stay1444.ARClens`), and install into the same place as before.
The Flatpak now asks to show notifications (for condition alerts).

New in game and not yet tried there: the loading-screen card, condition
alerts, and the map-panel focus fix. Reports (with a screenshot) are very
welcome.

Update both the app and the overlay together: the protocol between them
changed (v21). Game data comes from
[RaidTheory/arcraiders-data](https://github.com/RaidTheory/arcraiders-data)
and [MetaForge](https://metaforge.app/arc-raiders). Fan-made, not
affiliated with Embark Studios.
